---

The Timeless EP is four songs. Trippy, Honey Bunny, Hey Sasquatch! and Chaotic were written between shows over two years and recorded at home in a single winter, in a room with one window and a rug that did most of the acoustic treatment.

The order took longer than the recording. Trippy opens because it was the first song finished and still sounds like a door opening. Honey Bunny follows because it is short and does not ask for much. Hey Sasquatch! is the loudest thing on the record and sits third so the ear has somewhere to go afterwards.

Chaotic closes. It is the longest track at four minutes and the only one with a full take from all three of us in the same room at the same time. Nothing after it would have made sense.

Audio goes live with the release in December. Until then, this is the whole record: four titles, 12:43.
