The Fearless are three people who kept meeting at other bands' shows. Jaime Pérez plays bass. Zahir Chacín sings and plays lead guitar. Adrián Furlan writes the songs and plays guitar.

We found each other in September 2024, first through the Vampr app and then in a rehearsal room in Valencia. The first session was mostly tuning. The second produced the bones of Trippy.

Jaime plays with a pick and a lot of patience. Zahir arrived with a voice that did not need a microphone in a room that size, and had a guitar part for everything within a week.

Drummers have come and gone; Murat Mumcuoglu played on the record. If you drum and like what you hear, the contact page is the way in.
